The Real Cost

In ag, a clogged nozzle doesn't miss just one plant — it misses the whole pass before anyone notices.

Your service department works the same way. A job not opened before the warranty clock runs out. A claim sitting a week past the submission window. A machine 50 hours past its service interval with nobody alerted. Small, quiet losses running through every planting and harvest season — and most dealers never see exactly where it's going.

20 hrs

Combine Inspection — Pure Labor

No parts. Just 20 flat-rate hours of your technician's time — the kind of work that helps keep staff busy, especially during the slow season. Miss the submission and that labor cost is yours. But the bigger win: a completed inspection finds warranty-covered problems before the clock runs out, opening the door to even more covered repairs at standard service rates.

$1,350+

The Cost of a Missed Service Claim

A standard service runs 6–9 hours of shop time — $1,350+ in labor alone, before parts. The work is done. The parts are in. If the claim never makes it out the door, that reimbursement is gone.

2–3 jobs

Stop 2–3 Leaks. Varro Pays for Itself.

Recovering just a handful of missed service submissions annually covers the full cost of the software. That's before counting combine inspections, rogator services, or planter work.

The fleet math: A 150-machine dealership averaging 2 service events per year = 300 jobs annually. At a 3% miss rate, that's 9 jobs. At $1,350+ per job in labor and parts at standard service rates — that's $12,000+ walking out the door. And that doesn't count the warranty repairs an inspection would have caught while coverage was still active.

How Varro Works

Three main steps.
One system.
Nothing left behind.

Every feature in Varro is built around one goal — making sure nothing slips between the cracks of your service department.

Step 1 — Track

Machine Management & Job Opening

Know what you have, where it stands, and what needs to happen next.

  • Add and manage your equipment fleet
  • Track machine hours and warranty status
  • Hourly, annual, and inspection maintenance pages
  • Open jobs and assign them into the workflow
  • Alerts when machines approach service intervals
  • Full service history per machine
Owned by: Service Manager
Step 2 — Dispatch

Job Tracker & Technician Coordination

Make sure every open job gets done — and nothing stalls in the queue.

  • Job Tracker view for all active work orders
  • Assign and reassign technicians to jobs
  • Track job progress from open to complete
  • Technician portal — assigned machines & jobs
  • Visibility into workload across the team
  • No job falls through without a record
Owned by: Service Coordinator
Step 3 — Submit

Warranty Submission & Reimbursement

Get paid for the work your team already did — before the deadline.

  • Warranty submission tracking per job
  • Flag jobs approaching submission deadlines
  • Document everything needed before submitting
  • Reduce denied claims with complete records
  • Audit trail for every submission
  • Stop eating costs that should be reimbursed
Owned by: Warranty Clerk
Plus, management gets the full picture at a glance. The Fleet Dashboard shows machine health, warranty status, and service alerts across your entire operation. The FSM page gives leadership direct oversight of field service activity — no spreadsheets required.
